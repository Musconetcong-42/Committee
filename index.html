Rental terms & agreement

Rental rate & payment

The hourly rate is $150.00/hr or a flat rate as agreed. Payment is due within 3 days of invoice by cash, check, or digital transfer. Overtime beyond the agreed rental period is charged at $150.00/hr pro-rated.

Deposit

A cleaning deposit of $250.00 is due at the time of signing. The deposit is refundable or non-refundable as indicated on this form. The deposit will be returned within a reasonable time following the event, provided the Venue is returned in its original condition.

Cancellation policy

The Renter may cancel without charge with 30 or more days written notice. Cancellations made 15 days prior to the event will be charged 50% of the rental rate plus any expenses incurred by the Lodge in good-faith preparation. Cancellations within 5 days will be charged 100% of the rental cost plus all expenses.

Late fees

Payments not received within the required timeframe will incur either a flat fee of $25.00 per day or interest at 21% per annum compounding monthly, as selected on this form.

Maximum occupancy

A maximum of 200 attendees is permitted at all times due to fire hazard regulations. Violation of this limit constitutes immediate default and terminates this Agreement.

Cleanup responsibility

The Renter is responsible for cleaning the Venue after the event. The Venue must be returned in the same condition as at the start of the Rental Period. Failure to do so may result in forfeiture of the deposit.

Insurance

Where required, the Renter must obtain a Comprehensive General Liability Insurance Policy of not less than $1,000,000.00 covering bodily injury, property damage, and contractual liability. The Lodge shall be named as an additional insured and a Certificate of Insurance must be provided before the Rental Period begins.

Hold harmless

The Renter shall be liable for any physical damages to the Venue, legal actions, and/or loss of reputation or business opportunities that the Lodge may incur as a result of the Renter's or their guests' actions during the Rental Period. The Renter agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the Lodge against any and all legal actions arising from use of the Venue.

Landlord's right to cancel

The Lodge reserves the right to cancel this Agreement with at least 30 days' written notice. If cancellation is not due to Renter breach, all amounts paid including the deposit will be refunded. In the event of natural disaster or acts of God, this Agreement shall be canceled and all amounts refunded.

Dispute resolution

Disputes shall first be resolved through good-faith negotiation for no less than 30 days. If unresolved, the parties agree to mediation with costs shared equally. If mediation fails, disputes shall be submitted to binding arbitration in the same jurisdiction as the Venue.

Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New Jersey.

Severability & entire agreement

If any provision is found unenforceable, remaining provisions remain in full effect. This Agreement supersedes all prior understandings between the parties.
